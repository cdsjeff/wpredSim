Model description

›

Local statistical model. Candidate words are ranked from a high-frequency lexical prior plus smoothed conditional probabilities derived from the previous 1, 2, and 3 completed words. Full vocabulary adds broad American-English dictionary coverage. When the longest context is sparse, the model backs off to shorter contexts while retaining a frequency prior so common short words remain competitive.

Character updates. While a word is being typed, candidates are filtered by the current prefix and re-ranked using the same contextual probabilities. Next-word prediction can be switched off independently; prefix-based word completion remains available.

Vocabulary coverage. Ranked scopes emphasize conversationally useful words; Full adds more than 60,000 additional American-English dictionary entries. Prefix completion searches the entire active scope. Multiword predictions require observed contextual transitions in the local n-gram corpus.

Phrase updates. Multiword candidates are generated by extending high-ranked next words through the model. Phrase suggestions therefore change with both the typed prefix and the preceding context.

Prototype v0.18. Typed commas and terminal punctuation are preserved as target characters, counted as ordinary keyboard activations, and treated as lexical boundaries for prediction. Metrics are intended for teaching and simulation. “KSR” uses the current text length as the no-prediction character baseline and counts each physical key activation or prediction selection as one activation; correction keys therefore can produce negative KSR. Prediction misses are Test-mode target words completed without ever appearing in the prediction window during their composition; miss rate = misses ÷ target words evaluated. Predicted after typing counts completed target words whose first appearance in the prediction window occurred only after one or more letters of that word had been typed; predicted-after-typing rate = those words ÷ completed target words that appeared in the prediction window at some point. Predicted but typed counts completed target words that appeared in the prediction window but were completed by ordinary typing rather than prediction selection; prediction non-use rate = predicted-but-typed words ÷ completed target words that were available as predictions. Target availability is also shown as a decision-point percentage. Test targets can be pasted directly or loaded from plain-text .txt/.md files. Test statistics freeze when the participant explicitly finishes the trial; an exact target match is not required, so unnoticed errors remain in the final response. The three right-side panes use single-open accordion behavior. Live target progress and Test History can be shown or hidden independently, and the prediction window stays docked to the bottom of the viewport in Practice and Test modes.
